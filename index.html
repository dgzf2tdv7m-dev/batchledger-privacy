Batch Ledger Privacy Policy

Batch Ledger is a paid offline app. Notes, pinned cards, and saved records are stored locally on the device using Apple system storage.

No account or cloud sync

The app does not create accounts, upload user content, show ads, use analytics, use third-party SDKs, or sync data to cloud services.

Independent notebook

Batch Ledger is an independent record notebook and does not provide accounting, tax, compliance, or inventory management advice.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@example.com.

Batch Ledger is an independent local record-keeping app.
